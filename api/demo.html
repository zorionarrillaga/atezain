A record of each decision

The chain detects changes relative to its saved head. Keep an exported head outside this service to check against it later. An operator who controls the database can rewrite an unanchored chain.

Workspace access

An access token is a credential. Create a separate token for each reviewer or viewer. The audit identifies the token that approved a change; this workspace does not verify a person's identity through SSO.

Stop new work

Stopping blocks proposals and execution. A stopped workspace can be reopened no earlier than the following day. Records and exports stay accessible.

Delete workspace data

Export anything you need to keep. Deletion removes active records, the approval queue, audit history, checkpoints and access tokens. Backups follow the operator's retention policy.

Only the workspace owner can manage access or delete data.
Permissions and limits · what this assistant may do
Atezain is a supervised evaluation workspace. Drafts may repeat unsupported claims from customer messages. Check them against your source records before approving or sending. No email delivery, payment processing, SSO or compliance certification is provided here.
